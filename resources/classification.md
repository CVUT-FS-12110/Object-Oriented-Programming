# Credit (Zápočet)

The credit consists of two parts: Moodle tests and a semestral project.

### Moodle tests

Every week from the 2nd to the 7th week of the semester, students will take a Moodle test. There are 6 tests in total and up to 6 pts. can be scored for each test.

- possible maximum: 36 points (6 tests × 6 points)

### Semestral project

In the second half of the semester students will be assigned with a semestral project. **The semestral project is mandatory** - without submitting it by the deadline the credit cannot be awarded (regardless of the points from Moodle tests). The project will be defended at the exam (see Oral exam - instructor).

- possible maximum: 64 points
- no required minimum of points, but mandatory submission by the deadline: **18. 12. 2026**

### Credit requirements

The credit will be awarded upon submitting the semestral project and achieving at least 50 pts. from Moodle tests and semestral project combined.

| Category          | Possible MAX | Required MIN |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Moodle tests      | 36           | -            |
| Semestral project | 64           | submitted    |
| **Total**         | **100**      | **50**       |

# Exam

The exam is separated into three parts: theory test (20 pts.) and two oral exams: one covering lecture topics (20 pts.) and second covering lab topics (60 pts.).

### Theory test

Multiple choice tests covering topics from lectures and recommended study materials.

- possible maximum: 20 points
- required minimum: 10 points (50%)

### Oral exam - lecturer

Oral exams with the lecturer may cover any topic from lectures and recommended study materials. Special focus will be given to topics from Final State Examination (SZZ, Státní závěrečné zkoušky).

- possible maximum: 20 points
- required minimum: 10 points (50%)

### Oral exam - instructor

Oral exams with the instructor may cover any topic from labs, Moodle tests, semestral project and recommended study materials. Part of this exam is the defense of your semestral project. Special focus will be given to practical knowledge of Python language.

- possible maximum: 60 points
- required minimum: 30 points (50%)

## Additional information about the semestral project

- Upload your solution via Moodle assignments feature before the deadline (18. 12. 2026).
- Your submission has to be runnable by Python 3.x (You can of course use jupyter notebook (files `.ipynb`) during development, but the final solution has to be submitted as `.py` file(s)).
- If you use different than distribution packages, provide a requirements file for easy installation of the packages via Pip.
- Your code has to result in the expected output without any modification required from the lecturer (debugging, uncommenting). A project that cannot be run will be awarded 0 points.
- Your code needs to be commented and/or self-explanatory (final word has lecturer)
- Submission of solutions in weird formats (pictures, .txt, binary files) or solutions throwing errors will be considered a bad joke and can result in awarding an unlimited negative number of points.
- A lecturer can also award you with an undefined negative number of points if the lecturer finds your solution not original enough, or malignant in any way (final word has lecturer).
- You should be able to explain and defend your solution to prove that you are the author of the solution. Otherwise, the solution may not be accepted and you can be awarded negative points (the final word has lecturer).
